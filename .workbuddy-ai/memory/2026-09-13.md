## Rot repair (same day, follow-up)

All three flagged rows were verified and repaired. Registry 52 → **51**;
`FREE_CHAIN` 47 → **44**.

- **`lepton` — REMOVED from the registry entirely.** Lepton AI announced
  cessation of operations **2025-05-20** after the NVIDIA acquisition (5
  independent sources); `api.lepton.ai/v1/models` fails at the transport layer.
  Removed from union, `PROVIDER_IDS`, `PROVIDERS`, both `auth.ts` tables,
  `FREE_CHAIN`. A stale `leptonApiKey` in credentials.json is ignored.
- **`chutes` — left `FREE_CHAIN`.** Their own 2026-03 post says they removed
  ~20B tokens/day of sponsored free tier; pricing page is pay-per-1M-tokens,
  no subscription. **Also corrected the base URL: their docs use
  `llm.chutes.ai`, not `api.chutes.ai`** (kept as `fallbackBaseUrls`).
- **`cerebras` — left `FREE_CHAIN`.** Announced a points/credit model (Aug 2026):
  grant needs a verified payment method, expires in 30 days, then bills pay-go.
  Also removed from the `$0` list in `costNote()` (index.ts) and dropped
  `"cerebras:qwen-3-coder-480b": $0` from `PRICE_PER_1K` (router.ts) — that
  sticker had become fiction.

**Rule this establishes (worth keeping):** `--free` is documented as "never bills
pay-go", so a provider whose free tier now *requires a payment method* must leave
`FREE_CHAIN` even while it still answers. It can stay a builtin, reachable via
`--provider`. Correctness of the meter outranks the size of the chain.

Three regression tests pin this: `free-providers.test.ts` ("retired free tiers
stay out of the chain"), `router.test.ts` ("stops pricing cerebras $0"),
plus the count assertions.

Verified: lint clean, typecheck clean, build clean, `npm test` **272/272 pass**.
CLI smoke-checked: 51 providers listed, `lepton` absent, none of the three in
`free` output.

**Still open:** local runtimes (router's `private → local` route returns
"not wired"; `custom-providers.ts` rejects `http://`), and live-probing the 18
new `defaultModel` slugs.
