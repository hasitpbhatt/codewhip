# Paper: "Your Agent's Memory Is Its ACL — Interaction-Derived Authority and Resource Policy for LLM Agents"

## Thesis
Agent governance frameworks (Progent, AgentSpec) enforce hand-authored rules; memory literature warns against trusting learned rules but measures nothing; overthinking work covers reasoning tokens in single calls, not agentic loops. Codewhip governs BOTH dimensions harness-side from operator interaction: authority (declines → compiled pre-flight denies; approvals → remembered allows) and resources (mid-run token budgets, delegation budget-splitting, repeat-call memo+nudge, per-call deadlines) — all on a tamper-evident ledger. First measurement of the lifecycle, including the attacks each dimension creates.

## Research questions
- **RQ1 — Injection resistance of mined authority policy:** indirect injections planted in file contents; attack-success rate under prompt-only policy vs compiled harness denies (content-blind, pre-flight). Ground truth from the decision log.
- **RQ2 — The attack surface of interaction-derived policy itself:** (a) approval-persistence attacks (injection → one fat-fingered `a` → dangerous shape remembered across sessions = persistent privilege escalation — the new attack class); (b) decline-poisoning (minted denies = operator DoS). Ablate the anti-poisoning thresholds (3+ declines, 2+ runs, per-run dedup, provenance hashes) as the measured defense.
- **RQ3 — Generalization of mined deny shapes:** held-out dangerous variants vs benign near-misses for the shape grammar (exact / head-glob / origin); precision-recall of mined rules — motivates learned generalizers as future work.
- **RQ4 — Resource governance as containment:** mid-run token budget + per-call deadlines as the blast-radius limit for compromised or pathological agents. Measure economic damage of an injected agent (exfil loops, runaway fetches) with vs without budget enforcement; in delegation, child budget-splitting vs shared-budget snapshot (the 4× fan-out exposure we fixed is literally an ablation arm).
- **RQ5 — Agentic overthinking:** characterize repeat-call/step-burn pathologies on real tasks; measure the repeat-guard memo + nudge and hard budget on the cost–success frontier. (Distinct from the reasoning-token "overthinking" literature — this is tool-loop waste.)
- **RQ6 — Ledger integrity:** verifyChain detects post-hoc tampering of evaluation logs; `--replay` reproduces decisions (reproducibility contribution).

## Related-work positioning (from the lit scan)
Progent (privilege DSL, hand-authored) · AgentSpec (runtime rules, hand-authored) · CaMeL (engineered capabilities) · AgentDojo + coding-agent injection benchmarks (84% attack success on agentic editors) · overthinking literature (reasoning tokens, not tool loops) · policy mining (RBAC/ABAC, never transferred to agents) · memory-governance position papers (warnings, no measurements).
